Inventory Replenishment and Stockout Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT AN OFFICIAL RETAIL POLICY

1. Reorder thresholds
For the synthetic demonstration network, store shelf stock is reviewed when available units fall below 20 and warehouse stock below 35. The SQL table inventory.reorder_point is authoritative per location and can override these default planning examples.

2. Product P100 incident
Product P100 has a recurring shortage simulation caused by high demand and delayed replenishment purchase orders. Investigate recent sales velocity, supply lead time, warehouse holdings, pending purchase orders and store transfer possibilities before concluding a root cause.

3. Replenishment checklist
Confirm the SKU, store code, available stock and pending purchase orders; compare seven-day demand with forecast, identify receiving delays, request warehouse transfer where eligible, and record the replenishment owner and target date.
