Customer Service and Returns Manual
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT AN OFFICIAL RETAIL POLICY

1. Customer complaints
Log product SKU, complaint description, customer reference, opening date and resolution owner. Do not assume that repeated complaints prove a defect without investigation.

2. Return acceptance
Store teams inspect returned goods, verify the purchase and record the quantity, date, reason and SKU in the returns system. Refund decisions must follow the active local store returns terms.

3. Return rate analysis
For the academic dataset, return rate is calculated as returned units divided by sold units during the requested period; results over 100 percent can occur when return and sale windows differ and must be flagged.
