Merchandising and Shelf Execution Guidelines
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT AN OFFICIAL RETAIL POLICY

1. Planogram execution
Category Managers provide shelf layouts based on category strategy, sell-through and available capacity. Stores report any deviation in the execution tracker.

2. Availability and promotions
Promotion shelves are checked before launch against approved stock allocations. Low-stock promoted products are escalated for substitution approval.

3. Daily merchandising review
Review sales mix, item availability and return signals before requesting changes to shelf presentation.
