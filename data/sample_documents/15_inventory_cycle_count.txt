Inventory Cycle Count and Variance Procedure
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT AN OFFICIAL RETAIL POLICY

1. Daily counts
Count high-risk and low-stock SKUs each morning. Record counted quantity, system quantity, variance, verifier and timestamp.

2. Variance investigation
Investigate differences involving receiving errors, transfers, theft suspicions and return adjustments. Do not modify stock before supervisory approval.

3. Variance closure
Attach approved documentation and update warehouse or store inventory through the controlled inventory system.
