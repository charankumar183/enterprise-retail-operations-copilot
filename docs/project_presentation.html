The Enterprise Business Challenge

Modern retail conglomerates struggle with vast silos dividing unstructured operational guidelines and relational transactional ledgers:

  • Information Fragmentation: Store associates and supply chain managers spend 4-6 hours weekly navigating disconnected PDF manuals, supplier SLAs, and receiving protocols.
  • Chronic Stockout Revenue Loss: High-velocity FMCG products go out of stock due to demand spikes and unflagged supplier shipment delays.
  • Delayed PO Blindspots: Late shipments often go undetected until regional store shelves are depleted, leading to customer churn.
  • Policy Non-Compliance: Promotional discounts and markdown rules are inconsistently executed across store locations.

The Solution: Unified Enterprise AI Copilot

An intelligent assistant that unifies structured relational SQL data and unstructured enterprise documentation:

🔗 Dual Intelligence Engine

Seamlessly routes queries between live PostgreSQL analytical queries and multi-format document retrieval.

📑 Verifiable Source Citations

Every response is audited with Document Name, Section Reference, Page Number, and mathematical Confidence Score.

🛡️ Zero Hallucination Tolerance

Context compression and grounded SQL validation guarantee factual accuracy for operational decisions.

⚡ Low-Latency Architecture

End-to-end response times under 60 ms for complex multi-warehouse inventory and policy lookups.

10 Mandatory Advanced RAG Capabilities

1. Ingestion & Intelligent Chunking

Native parsing for PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, and Excel. Hierarchical recursive character + metadata chunking.

2. Parent-Child Retrieval

Indexes dense 350-character child chunks in ChromaDB; resolves 1,500-character parent blocks for full LLM context.

3. Query Expansion & Multi-Query

Expands retail queries with domain synonyms; executes parallel hybrid retrieval with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.

4. Hybrid Search (Dense + BM25)

Combines semantic vector embeddings with BM25 keyword matching (α = 0.60) for exact SKU and vendor code recall.

Agentic Intent Routing (Without LangGraph)

A deterministic, low-latency intent router dynamically directs queries to specialized execution pipelines:

  • RAG Search Route: Retrieves inbound receiving SOPs, vendor master agreements, return policy manuals, and promotion guidelines.
  • SQL Analytics Route: Executes parameterized PostgreSQL queries for real-time inventory balances, supplier lead times, and return rates.
  • Report Generator Route: Formulates automated daily store operations summaries and Store 210 replenishment checklists.
  • Combined Analytics & RAG: Correlates live inventory shortages with policy SOP thresholds for root-cause diagnosis.
